girgit /ˈɡɪr.ɡɪt/ noun Hindi

  1. 1

    A chameleon.

  2. 2

    (as an insult) Someone who will not stay the same. Girgit ki tarah रंग बदलना: to change colour like a chameleon.

    (our meaning) Attention. A chameleon answers to where it is. Nothing about it is fixed except the attention.
