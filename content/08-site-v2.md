# Site v2 — content first (draft for sign-off)

A one-page site in the spirit of the references (Lumen Cat, Dino Lab, the Susan
Kare longread). The chameleon is the mascot and lives in every section. Hand-drawn
doodles, sticker tags and handwritten asides sit on a clean grid.

**Copy rules:** everything follows `00-voice-and-hindi-system.md`. Headlines marked
✅ come straight from the approved copy (`01-home.md`). Lines marked ✏️ are new
and need your OK. `[[…]]` is information only you have.

Every section has three parts:
- **Words:** the copy.
- **Aside:** the small handwritten line in brackets, the reference sites' "(what we do)" device. Short, dry, never cute for its own sake.
- **Graphics + interaction:** what to draw and what it does. Notes only; designed after the words are signed off.

## 2. The name *(the fold people screenshot)*

**Words (✅ approved, unchanged):**
> In Hindi, calling someone a **गिरगिट** is not a compliment.
>
> *Girgit ki tarah* **रंग बदलना**: to change colour like a chameleon. It's what you call a person who won't stay the same.
>
> **We took the name anyway.**
>
> Because changing colour isn't dishonesty. It's attention. A chameleon answers to where it is. Nothing about it is fixed except the attention.
>
> That's the whole job. The work should belong to you: carry your **मिज़ाज**, your market, your ambition. Not our portfolio.

The chameleon line uses the approved biology-safe alternate, not "set against bark reads as bark".

**Aside:** ✏️ (yes, it's an insult. we checked.)

**Graphics + interaction:**

- **Hindi:** hover or tap a Hindi word and it swaps to English, as in the voice guide.

## 5. How we work (the seven days)

**Aside:** ✏️ (how the week actually runs)

**Headline:** ✅ **Seven days, from approved scope to live.**

**Line:** ✅ Not a rough first draft. A website you would be happy to send to an investor on day eight.

**Cards** (tilted, hand-labelled, numbered like Lumen Cat's process cards):
- **Before the clock:** ✅ content collected, references studied, sitemap agreed, visual direction set, scope signed off.
- **Day 1:** ✅ direction, on screen, not in a deck.
- **Day 3:** ✅ the real thing, in a browser, on your phone.
- **Day 7:** ✅ live.

**Small print:** ✅ One revision round is included. If it's genuinely bigger than seven days, we say so before you pay anything, ship a complete V1 in seven, and take the rest into Phase 2.

**Graphics + interaction:**
- **The chameleon:** walks a hand-drawn line from card to card as you scroll.
- **The day stamp:** each card gets a stamp ("DAY 3 ✓") as you reach it.
- **Card tops:** handwritten labels, Lumen-style, such as "pre-production" or "day 3".

## 6. Why the speed is real

**Aside:** ✏️ (fast, not cheap. different thing.)

**Headline:** ✅ **Fast isn't a discount.**

**Three columns (✅ approved):**
- **A small senior team.** Three or four people, all senior, one owner per project. Nothing waits in a queue behind someone else's approval.
- **No handoff.** The designer builds the frontend. The person who drew it ships it.
- **The slow part happens first.** Research, references, sitemap, direction and scope are settled before day one. Seven days of building, not seven days of deciding.

**Graphics + interaction:** each column gets one doodle: three heads, one arrow that doesn't break, an hourglass already empty. Each draws itself on scroll.

## Decided (2026-10-24)

1. **Hero headline:** "You missed it. We didn't." The approved house-style line becomes the sub.
2. **Graphic style:** hand-drawn marker doodles with a few pixel accents for surprise.
3. **Accent colour:** louder than burnt orange, reference-style, chosen to sit with the logo's ink and paper. The exact colour is to be proposed.
4. **The chameleon:** a flat drawn character in the doodle style, not 3D.
