# Start a Project

Not a contact page. A qualifying brief — it should collect enough that the first
reply can be substantive, and enough that you can decline the wrong projects
without a call.

Every other page on the site points here. It's the only CTA.

---

## Hero

> **Eyebrow** — `[[Currently taking projects for MONTH]]`

> # <span class="hi" data-en="begin">शुरू</span> Let's start.

> Tell us what you're building. You'll hear back within one working day from
> the person who'd run the project — with a real response, not an
> acknowledgement.

**Notes.** शुरू sits directly before "Let's start," which translates it in
place. It's the page's only Hindi word.

The availability eyebrow is worth maintaining. "Currently taking projects for
March" creates honest urgency and signals a real studio with a real calendar.
It has to be true and it has to be updated — a stale month is worse than no
eyebrow.

## The form

Short enough to finish, long enough to qualify. Seven fields.

> **What are you building?** *(required)*
> A sentence is fine. A paragraph is better.
> `[[textarea]]`
>
> **What do you need from us?** *(required, multi-select)*
> A website · A product or web app · Branding · Video · A UX audit ·
> Design capacity for my agency · Not sure yet
>
> **When do you need it?** *(required)*
> As soon as possible · Within a month · Within three months · Just exploring
>
> **Budget range** *(required)*
> `[[Under $X]]` · `[[$X–$Y]]` · `[[$Y–$Z]]` · `[[Over $Z]]` · Not sure yet
>
> **Do you have a logo and brand identity?**
> Yes · No · Sort of
>
> **Links** — your current site, references you like, anything relevant.
> `[[text]]`
>
> **Name and email** *(required)*

> **→ Send**

> Prefer email? `[[hello@girgitlabs.com]]`
> `[[Or book a 20-minute call →]]`

**Notes on the fields.**

**Budget is required and it's the most important field.** Every instinct says
making it optional will get more submissions. It will — and most of the extra
ones will be unqualified, and you'll spend the calls discovering it. A budget
band is the difference between a lead list and an inbox. Include "Not sure yet"
so genuine early-stage founders aren't blocked; the ones who select it and write
a serious brief are worth talking to.

**"Do you have a logo?" pre-empts the seven-day process's hardest dependency.**
Answering "no" here means the first reply can already explain that branding is
separate work, before anyone's expectations are set.

**Seven fields is the ceiling.** Every field past this point costs you good
leads. If you want more, ask on the call.

**No phone number field.** It suppresses international submissions and you
don't need it to reply.

## Below the form

> ## What happens next
>
> **1. You hear back within one working day.** From `[[Karmaveer]]`, with an
> actual response to what you sent.
>
> **2. A short call.** Twenty to thirty minutes. What you're building, what
> success looks like, whether we're the right studio for it. We'll tell you if
> we're not.
>
> **3. A proposal.** Fixed scope, fixed price, fixed dates. Usually within two
> working days of the call.
>
> **4. Groundwork.** Content, references, sitemap, direction, scope sign-off.
>
> **5. The clock starts.**

**Notes.** Publishing the sequence removes the anxiety that stops people
submitting forms — they don't know what they're triggering. Five numbered steps
says "this is a process, not a sales trap."

Step 2's "we'll tell you if we're not" is the highest-trust sentence available
to a small studio and costs nothing. Only publish it if it's true.

## Form states

Every one of these is real copy that needs writing. Most agency sites leave them
as browser defaults and it's the fastest way to look unfinished on the one page
that matters most.

> **Sending** — Sending…
>
> **Success** — Got it. `[[Karmaveer]]` will reply within one working day —
> check your spam folder if you don't hear from us, and email
> `[[hello@girgitlabs.com]]` if it's urgent.
>
> **Error** — That didn't send. Try again, or email us directly at
> `[[hello@girgitlabs.com]]` — we'd rather you did that than lose the message.
>
> **Required field** — We need this one.
>
> **Invalid email** — That email doesn't look right — check it?
>
> **Rate limited / duplicate** — Looks like that already came through. We have
> it.

**Notes.** The success message must name a person and a timeframe. "Thanks,
we'll be in touch" is the sound of a form that goes nowhere.

Always give the direct email in the error state. A failed form on your only
conversion page is the most expensive bug on the site — the fallback has to be
right there.
