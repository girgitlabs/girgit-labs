# Capabilities

This page exists for one reason: to stop Girgit being read as a website factory.
The homepage sells seven days. This page shows the studio behind it.

Pentagram's taxonomy is the model — a broad, confident list with no apology for
its breadth. The name has already justified it.

---

## Hero

> **Eyebrow** — Capabilities

> # Tell us what you need.

> The seven-day website is the front door. Most people who come through it stay
> for something else.

**Notes.** The founder's working philosophy is "Tell us what you need. We'll
build it." As a homepage headline I argued against it — it's what every
commodity shop says. Here, on a page that then proves the range, the first half
works: it's an invitation with evidence directly underneath. Dropping "We'll
build it" is deliberate. The promise is in the list, and an unqualified "we'll
build anything" undercuts the premium positioning the doc asks for.

## The list

Grouped in four. Each group: a heading, a line, and the specifics.

### Interfaces

> Screens people use for hours, not seconds. Web apps, dashboards, tools,
> internal systems — the work where usability is the whole product and the
> visual design has to survive real data.
>
> Digital product design · Web applications · Dashboards & data interfaces ·
> Design systems · Mobile and app design · Prototyping

### Websites

> Marketing sites, portfolios, catalogues, launches. Designed and built by the
> same people, which is why they arrive fast and why they look in the browser
> the way they looked in the file.
>
> Seven-day websites · Multi-page marketing sites · Ecommerce front ends ·
> Frontend implementation · Motion & interaction · Deployment & hosting ·
> Backend integration, with our development partner

### Brand

> Identity for companies that will mostly be seen on a screen. Built as a
> system from the first sketch, because a logo that only works on a business
> card is a liability now.
>
> Brand identity · Naming support · Visual systems & guidelines ·
> Graphic design · Packaging & print · Presentation and pitch design

### Motion & AI

> Two capabilities most studios our size don't have in-house, and the reason
> some projects come to us instead of to a bigger name.
>
> Video editing · Videography · Motion graphics · Product films ·
> AI-enabled interfaces & experiences · AI-assisted production

**Notes on the Motion & AI group.** Harsh's video work is a genuine
differentiator and grouping it with AI gives it weight it wouldn't have as a
line item at the bottom of a design list. Keep the AI description at exactly
this volume — the operating doc is explicit that AI strengthens execution and
is demonstrated through the work rather than announced. Two lines is
demonstration-adjacent. Two paragraphs would be a claim.

## Consulting

> ## When you don't need us to build anything
>
> **UX audit.** We use your product the way your users do, and write down
> everything that's costing you. Usually 15 to 30 findings, ranked by what it
> would cost to fix against what it would earn. No pitch attached.
>
> **UX consultation.** Working sessions with your team — on structure, on
> flows, on a decision you're stuck between.
>
> **Design capacity for agencies.** White-label, under your name, on your
> timeline. We're comfortable being invisible.

**Notes.** The audit is the single best door-opener in the operating doc's
original playbook — the whole audit-note outreach method depends on it existing
as a real, priced product. Give it a page of its own once the offer is defined.

The agency line matters: agencies are a named target client and they will not
enquire unless the site explicitly says you'll stay behind the curtain.

## How engagements work

> ## Three shapes
>
> **Sprint** — one defined thing, fixed price, fixed date. The seven-day
> website is the best-known one. Most first projects are this.
>
> **Project** — bigger scope, phased, with checkpoints. A product, a rebrand, a
> platform.
>
> **Partner** — a monthly retainer where we're the design team. For companies
> shipping continuously who don't want to hire in-house yet.
>
> Everything is fixed price. We don't bill by the hour — it punishes you for our
> learning curve and rewards us for being slow.

**Notes.** That last sentence is the most quotable line on the page and it's a
real argument, not a slogan. Keep it.
